Constellation Authentication

Sign in as a Constellation — this checks your username and password directly against its record under ORV/Constellations, the same way the main game checks player logins. Signing in doesn't wake the Constellation up: it's already sponsoring and watching in the background whether or not anyone is signed in as it. Signing in just means you're the one at the wheel for rewards and messages right now. Evernight perms (full roster + account management) depend on that record's evernight flag.

☾ Constellation Registry

Only visible to the Evernight seat. Each Constellation is one record: name, trait, username, and password. Edit a row and press Save, reset a password by typing a new one, and grant or revoke Evernight. Leave username/password blank to keep a Constellation as a background-only presence — it still sponsors and rewards Incarnations, it just has no one signed in as it.
